## Part 1 — Title (elevator)

> **"Bahasa Indonesia classroom platform with a proven artifact-driven activation loop. Pre-revenue, now proving repeatability before raising."**

## Part 2 — Problem

Indonesian teachers — especially Bahasa Indonesia (B. Indonesia / BIN) subject teachers across SMP/SMA — have no dedicated digital home to run **live** language classrooms. Generic classroom LMS tools focus on generic homework and grading; they ignore what a Bahasa Indonesia teacher actually needs: **writing/literacy exercises, karya (creative work) publication, feedback, gamified practice, and UKBI/TKA-aligned assessment**.

## Part 3 — Solution

One platform that connects the whole loop:

- **For teachers** — run live classes: penugasan (assignments), bank-soal quizzes (Latihan Harian), karya/literacy publishing with feedback, and AI tools (RPP, Soal, PPT, EYD, feedback, grading).
- **For students** — a gamified learning path: Jalur Cerdas (Duolingo-style), XP/rank/badges, Karya social feed, plus UKBI/TKA simulations and Dokumen Hasil Latihan.
- **For assessment** — UKBI/TKA simulation (810+ UKBI, 200+ TKA soal), AI evaluation center.
