# Processing inventory and retention ownership
| Processing | Data / subjects | Purpose and basis to validate | Access | Retention / action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Account/auth | Email identity DOB, adult and child | Account necessity; guardian consent for child data | Owner, authorized operators, Supabase | Active account; tombstone on verified deletion; auth refresh revoked |
| Guardian evidence | Guardian email identity child band notice proof | Child protection and provable consent | Wali and independent reviewer | Undelivered invitation 7-day expiry; stale contacts erased after 30 days; consent minimum annual necessity review |
| Learning/class/work | Answers progress works recordings | Educational service and verified child consent | Owner, assigned teacher, authorized operator | Active account; personal content deleted on account closure; review inactive accounts yearly |
| Public publication | Adult profile works public rankings | Separate reversible adult opt-in | Public | Disable immediately on withdrawal; child private always |
| AI | Minimised prompts answers audio suggestions | Separate optional consent; child wali permission; approved transfer | Assigned teacher, approved API provider | Jobs/saved results 30 days; pedagogical assessment evidence reviewed independently; no automatic final grading |
| Analytics | Allowlisted optional product events | Adult optional consent | Restricted operator | 90 days; disabled for children |
| Payments | Orders payer contact finance records | Contract and required bookkeeping | Restricted finance/vendor | Statutory documents generally 10 years where applicable; anonymised app identity; reviewed lawful hold |
| Reports/security | Content locator optional contact decision log | Safety and legitimate/legal handling | Restricted moderator/incident owner | Resolved payload 180 days; active escalations case-reviewed; logs target30days and vendor expiry verification |
| Backup/export | Prior copies of above | Availability/recovery necessity | Restricted operator/vendor | Confirm vendor cycle; deletion replay before restore; inventory exports |

Lawful basis is purpose-specific: accepting a notice is acknowledgment, not a blanket consent for advertising, public disclosure, third-party training or unrelated processing. Rights requests: register reference/time/identity assurance/purpose, assess authority, fulfill access/correction/restriction/withdrawal/deletion as applicable, document specific refusal/legal hold and appeal route. Do not send full datasets by unverified email. Maintain statutory deadlines from current UU PDP, rather than a generic support SLA.

Annual review: actual processing, minimisation, DPO/appointed responsible-person threshold, processor contracts, transfer safeguards (equivalent protection then binding safeguards then the permitted consent fallback under Pasal56), child risk and assurance, accessibility, independent human review and appeal. New profiling/AI purpose requires new DPIA and purpose-specific notice/consent.
